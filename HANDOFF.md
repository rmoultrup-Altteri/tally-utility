## Failed Approaches (Don't Repeat These)

- **Using the Agent tool with a placeholder prompt as a "wait for background task" mechanism** (2026-07-05): spawns a *real* agent that takes the literal text as its assignment.
- **Asserting a column has no CHECK constraint without grepping `<table>_<column>_check`** (2026-08-06). The constraint list sits ~50 lines below the column definition.
- **Editing a section's closing "Next:" line as the anchor for appending a new section** (2026-08-07). Anchor on the closing paragraph, re-emit it verbatim ahead of the new content, verify the seam. *(Sections T, U and V were appended safely that way.)*
- **Trusting a canonical invariant's `Schema enforcement status` field** (2026-08-07, 2026-08-09, 2026-08-10, again 2026-08-11). CI-077 asserts a table that does not exist; CI-120 is graded on a true-but-not-load-bearing clause; CI-118 is graded structural on a UNIQUE over a **nullable** column; CI-116's read-isolation clause is graded structural on a claim that is unconditionally false absent `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`. Grep `^CREATE TABLE public.<name>`, read the column definition, **check the nullability of every column in a cited UNIQUE**, and for any RLS claim **check for `FORCE` and remember RLS does not apply to a table's owner**.
- **Applying a correct method to an object you have not confirmed exists** (2026-08-12, new — and the most transferable lesson of the whole WU6 run). `gap-analysis-v5.2.1.md`'s CI-025 entry did exactly the right thing: it read the `enforce_reading_validation_workflow` trigger's locked-field whitelist and tested whether `read_type` was a member.
