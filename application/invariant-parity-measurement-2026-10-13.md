# Per-invariant schema parity measurement — 2026-10-13

**Purpose.** Step 1 of the core-build gate (schema at parity with the invariants and scenarios) had no finish line: the parity plan's status table was last checked 2026-09-23 and the register's grades are v5.2.1-era. This document measures each of the 135 canonical invariants (CI-001…CI-135, `gas-billing-memory/application/canonical-invariants.md`) against the **current** schema (tu.sql through v5.4.2-18 including -15; 130 tables; live `tally` build) so step 1 becomes a finite list.

**Method.** Seven read-only agents (Sonnet 5.5), one per block of families, each reading its CI entries and checking the live catalog, tu.sql, the patch files, DEPLOY-VERIFICATION, APPLICATION-CONTRACTS, DECISION-LOG, the parity plan and Kyle's decision/question files. Rules applied: the schema *represents* and *protects record integrity* (RLS, immutability, append-only, DB-stamped times, keys); the C# core *evaluates* (windows, caps, interest, eligibility, rule selection), so "represented as data, evaluated in core" is not a gap; a gap that waits on an unanswered ruling is BLOCKED, not GAP. **Check on the result:** 629 backticked object names in the evidence cells were matched against the live catalog; the unmatched ones are enum values, JSON keys, wildcards, roles, or objects the agent says are absent by design. **Known limits:** CI-047, the tax-exemption lifecycle call and the surcharge-line freeze call rest on names, constraints and column lists, not on trigger bodies (agent C); CI-119 (double billing across runs) was not traced to the end and is marked medium (agent F). **Not done:** the classifications themselves were not independently re-reviewed; each row carries the agent's confidence, and the `low`/`med` rows deserve a second look before a decision rests on them.

**Classes.** REP+PROT — represented and its integrity part enforced. REP — represented; the invariant is the core's/app's to evaluate (scenario material). PARTIAL — something specific is missing (named). GAP — not represented, nothing blocks building it. BLOCKED — cannot be built until a named ruling (Kyle / Ryan / the finance gate). N/A — not a schema matter.

**This document is the live parity record** (Ryan, 2026-10-15). The grades in `gas-billing-memory/application/canonical-invariants.md` are frozen as of v5.4.2-12 (2026-09-23); GBM's ingestion manifest points here. Re-grade here, in a dated update section, after each mirrored patch.
