Kyle, batch 5: tax by location. 10 questions; each says what we've written, so "yes" is enough where we have it right. The worked bills are in `scenarios/tax.md`. Neither of the Comptroller's rules we read (34 TAC §3.295 on gas and electricity, §3.334 on local taxes) answers questions 1 or 3, which is why they come to you.

*When a tax starts or changes*
1. (SCN-0108, 0109) A tax starts or changes on a quarter's first day (Apr 1), and a bill's period runs Mar 15 – Apr 14. Is the whole bill taxed, none of it (the first bill wholly after Apr 1 carries it), or the taxed lines split by days? We suggest the utility states its method when it enters the change, as you ruled for rate changes. OK?
2. (SCN-0109) The utility approves a published rate increase late, after bills went out at the old rate. It owes the higher tax anyway (our reading). Does it correct those bills, put the difference on the next bill, or absorb it? And if one of those bills is later voided and rebilled for another reason, we charge the new rate on the rebill. Right?
3. (SCN-0108) An annexed premise was charged city tax a quarter early by a clerk's mistake. By law (Tax Code §321.3025) that tax counts as the city's revenue, and the customer may claim a refund. We credit it to the customer when found. OK, or leave it to them to claim?

*Exemptions*
4. (SCN-0107) A certificate is verified in November, dated back to January. We don't change past bills by ourselves; an operator decides whether to credit the tax, and we record who decided. Is there a default, and how far back may the credit go? (The customer can also claim from the Comptroller, §3.295(h)(1).)
5. (SCN-0107) A certificate ends Oct 31, inside a period of Oct 15 – Nov 13. Is that bill exempt, taxed, or split?

*Residential or not*
6. (SCN-0111) One meter serves a home and a hair salon. The law taxes it all or nothing by predominant use each billing period. Who decides the use each period: the use the utility has on record for the premise (changed when the customer tells us), or a figure on each bill?
7. (SCN-0111) A house rented to visitors by the week: we tax it as non-residential (the visitors aren't tenants for over 29 days). Right?

*Penalties and payments*
8. (SCN-0104) A forfeited discount is "taxed like the gas". On a business's bill that means state and county tax too, not only city. Right?
9. (SCN-0114) A part payment reaches a bill's tax lines but can't pay them all. Paid in stack order (state first), or in proportion? And is tax "collected" in the month the payment posts, or the month of the bill it paid? (Which one the utility reports on is its own call; we keep both.)

*Figures*
