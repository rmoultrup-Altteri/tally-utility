## Blocking

### B1. A payment held as an overpayment credit that paid money out can never bounce (Opus B3, Fable B1; both executed)
Kyle's K-10 example through the credit: payment 60, held as a credit, refunded (or donated) from the credit, then the bank returns 60. The bounce is refused while the credit lives; the credit can't be voided while its posted refund counts.
**Fix (Fable's, measured):** the credit leg of the bounce guard and the nsf/reversed move waits only while an overpayment credit is applied, has a refund pending, or still holds money; what it already paid out stands, as a posted refund of the payment does. The guard takes each credit's lock after the payment's; the credit's void takes the payment's lock before its own (one order: payment, then credit). [K-10 1 = A; guard the act]

### B2. An escheated credit gets money back (Opus B1, Codex B1 second path, Codex B2; executed)
Three ways, none checking the credit's status:
- a donation undone after the escheat [fix: refused, as an application undo already is (R12j); Opus B3 O4b];
- a refund still pending at the escheat, then cancelled [fix: escheat waits until no refund of the credit is pending; the operator posts or cancels it first; K-10 2 by analogy];
- a refund that went out, whose check then bounces after the escheat. A real event; it cannot be refused. **ASK** where that money goes.

### B3. A donation's correction skips the credit's cap (Codex B1; executed)
Donate 100, undo it, refund 100, then reverse the undo: admitted, remaining −100 (multi-row and CTE too).
**Fix:** a row under a donation takes the credit's lock after the tree's. One that takes money from the credit again (a reversal of the undo) is a draw: `check_cash_source(..., 'donation', ...)`. One that gives money back is checked for an ended source and recorded order, not capped. [guard the act]

### B4. A posting row is admitted for a payment or refund that has ended (Opus B2, Fable S1; executed)
A voided payment reads 40 received; a cancelled refund's row posts and a second full refund is admitted (80 paid out of 50).
**Fix:** a posting row is admitted only while its payment is pending, read under the payment's lock (the move to posted or voided takes the same lock). [guard the act; count-guards need a lock handshake]

### B5. A draw may be dated before its source's arrival entry (Codex B3; executed)
A payment dated day 1 posted by an entry dated day 8; a refund dated day 5 reads remaining −30 with no bounce. Same for a credit.
**Fix:** the arrival entry is dated the source's money date (payment_date, issued_date), checked at its write. [one money date per fact]
