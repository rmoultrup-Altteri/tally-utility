## Blocking

### B1. With two holders of money beside a deficit, the covered one reads less than its caps admit (Fable B1, Opus B2, Codex B1; all executed)
The per-side caps count each side's shares as recorded; the pool holds the total; the cover hands a deficit to the latest holder. With one holder, the pool binds it exactly. With two, one reads less than it can draw. Fable P2: K 0, R1 20, R2 20 read; R2 admits 30. Opus G: the payment 50, K2 20; K2 admits 30, after which the payment reads 40 untouched. Codex 01: R2 reads 10, admits 20. No order of the cover fixes it (r6's order had it on the payment, unfound).
**Needs Ryan:** what a displayed credit balance means. Either:
- (a) narrow the claim: a position never exceeds its caps, the positions add up to the ledger, and no line reads money beside another's deficit. The comments (1915–1917, R17, R20) and agree() are narrowed, and P2, P3 and G are added (Fable's recommendation); or
- (b) make the per-side caps read the covered position at the draw's date, so each line pays out exactly what it shows (Opus's direction). This reverses r6's "a draw uses only its own side's money" between members.
