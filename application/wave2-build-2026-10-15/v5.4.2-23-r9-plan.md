### B2. A refund's return stamped before a take-back moves the take-back's share onto, or off, an escheated reissue after the fact (Opus B1; executed, real sessions)
A refund's return (its nsf row and the move to nsf) is stamped at its transaction's start (R17). One that began before a take-back and commits after it changes what its credit held at the take-back's instant, so `overpayment_shares` moves the take-back's share from a later reissue to the credit. Opus J: the reissue had been escheated in between and ends holding 10 of its own on a remitted credit; the utility can refund only 10 of the 20 it holds.
**Fix (author's call, follows r6's recorded-order rule for draws):** every *leaving move* on a family runs `check_cash_order`'s arrival-entry leg under the payment's lock it already takes, and is refused for retry (40001) when a row under the payment's posting entry (a take-back or give-back) was recorded after the move's stamp. Leaving moves: a refund of the payment or of a family credit moving to nsf, voided or reversed; a family credit's void (a voided reissue gives its money back to its predecessor at its own instant). The retry lands after the take-back, so the bank's return is still recorded; only its stamp moves. The shares as recorded then stay fixed once recorded, which the escheat move, the give-back guard, Z5 and the reissue's size already assume. R17 says "retried, not stamped back". Opus S3's second point (R17's "both readings are consistent") is rewritten with it.
- Tests: J as race CR27 (T's return retried with 40001; on retry the share stays on R and the return is settled); the void variant as CR28; a mutation dropping each new call.

### B3. Tests only: agree() no longer pins what a line can pay (Opus B2 = Fable S1; executed)
Under option A agree() checks the position admitted and the pool plus a cent refused. With two lines holding money nothing pins either line's payable figure; Opus's mA (the payment's cap subtracts the credit's share twice) and mB (the overpayment credit's cap forgets its share) pass all 390 checks, though under them the payment pays out 70 against its own 50, or the credit 50 against its own 30.
**Fix:** agree_side admits a refund of exactly `least(own cap, pool)` and refuses it plus a cent (a cent when the least is ≤ 0), for each active line, keeping its other legs. A credit's own cap is `customer_credit_own_remaining_as_of`; the payment's is computed in the battery as the cap computes it (received − applied − refunded − the posting entry's rows net of the family's shares). Fable's `agree_exact` (r8src/probe-r8.sql) is the template. Add mA and mB as mutations, anchored at the first agree() that sees each. The header can then say the exact thing: a line pays exactly the lesser of its own cap and the pool; its position is an attribution of at most that.
