## Blocking

1. **Releases are not ordered.** (Opus B1, Fable B1, Codex probe 03.) An undo or a misposting return could be recorded before what it releases. At the recorded instants in between, a receipt read 200 held of 100, or the same 100 read as held and as received by a live payment. **Fix (rule: -23's `check_cash_order`, applied to both sides).** Every disposition runs `check_suspense_recorded_order`, not only a positive one. The check also reads a reversed payment's recorded time.
2. **The order check skips a row of the other table that shares a uuid.** (Codex probe 02.) This is round 1's provider self-exclusion bug, repeated. **Fix.** The check excludes its own row only within its own table.
3. **A partly bounced payment's misposting return gave back the whole payment.** (Codex probe 01.) 40 was taken back by the bank and 60 reversed as misposted, but the return gave back 100. **Fix (rule: guard the act).** A misposting return gives back at most what the non-NSF rows reversing the payment's posting entry came to.
4. **NaN amounts.** (Codex probe 07; lesson `check-passes-null`: NaN is greater than every number.) A receipt, a disposition or a pledge of NaN was accepted, and a NaN receipt funded any payment. **Fix.** The amount CHECKs refuse NaN.

## Should fix

5. **The invariant's reach.** (Opus S1, Fable S1, Codex probe 06.)
   - **Bodies.** The md5 list adds `suspense_require_read_committed`, `suspense_today_latest`, `suspense_today_earliest`, `assistance_agreement_in_force`, the aged, held and open readers, and the two settings wrappers.
   - **Attributes.** A new exact list records each listed function's volatility, security and settings (the `search_path` pin).

   - **NOT NULL.** A new exact list records the NOT NULL columns of the seven tables. PostgreSQL 16 keeps them outside `pg_constraint`.
6. **A load with foreign keys off.** (Opus N.) The disposition and pledge-event guards check that the receipt or pledge is of the row's tenant, as the pledge guard checks its customer's tenant (r2).
