## By rule (author)

- **An undo dated in the future (Opus B1, executed).**
  - Today the return check reads an undo from its repayment's date, while the positions read it at its own date. An undo dated 2099 settles the return check, but the bounced payment reads the money until 2099, and nothing can repair it.
  - r3: the return check also reads every row at its own date from today on. An undo dated today or earlier settles it, with R5's interval only before today. A future-dated undo is refused.
  - *Why this, not refusing a future money date:* -23 never limits a money date to today. It limits recorded times, and caps what the dates add up to. This keeps to that.
- **A carry born ended (Codex B1, author-run: a history load's carry born `escheated`, holding 30, accepted).** A `shortfall_reissue` credit is born active, for every role, superuser included. Its endings go through -23's checked moves. The rule is added to the invariant, with a regression and a mutation.
- **A carry names an undo recorded after the escheat (Opus S1, executed).** In r2 a carry could name an older undo as a label for unrelated money, such as a returned refund, which could then never get its own reissue. r3 requires the undo's `created_at` to be at or after the credit's escheat (`escheated_at`). -23's `refund_reissue` may have the mirror gap. That is a note for the money stream, outside -27's tables.
