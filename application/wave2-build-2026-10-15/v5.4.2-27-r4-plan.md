## By rule (author)

- **An undo dated after today, recorded alone (Opus B1, Fable B1; both executed).**
  - r3's second reading only runs when money leaves the family. An undo dated 2099, recorded before the refund comes back, is admitted. When the refund check then comes back, its return is refused until 2099. An undo is never undone, a second undo goes over the repayment, and a new repayment fails the same reading, so nothing can make room.
  - r3's plan rejected refusing a future date because "-23 never limits a money date to today". That was wrong: -23 refuses a ledger reversal dated after today (`tu.sql` 43166, R-02, "an undo is of something that happened, dated no later than today").
  - r4: an undo of a shortfall application is dated no later than the later of today and the date of the row it undoes. A repayment dated in the future can still be undone on its own date.
  - "Today" stays the latest date anywhere (UTC+14), not -23's `CURRENT_DATE`: tenants have no zone (Opus N, Fable N5), and an undo the utility dates today in its own zone must never be refused. R5 says how this differs from -23.
  - With the date rule, r3's second reading can never refuse what the first admits: every undo is then dated on or before today, or on its own repayment's date. So the second reading and its zero row go, as a guard another check always beats (PATCH-LESSONS, rule-table-fixture-notes). Mutations M56, M57, M57b and M58 go with them, and new ones replace them.
  - Tests: R0, an undo dated 2099 recorded alone, refused; R0b, the day after today alone, refused; R0c, today alone, admitted; a repayment dated in the future undone on its own date, admitted, and one day later, refused. R1 and R1b stay; they are now refused at the undo's insert. A mutation for each side of the date rule.

## Settled: the undo a carry names, in one transaction (Fable S1, executed)

r3 compared the undo's recorded time with the escheat's (`<`); in one transaction they are equal, so an undo written while the credit was active could label a returned refund's money in a later carry. Put to Ryan as three options; he said go with the recommendation (option 1, strictly later), and asked why money-flow questions kept reaching him. This one decides only which record a carry names, so it is settled by rule (DECISION-LOG 2026-11-06).

**Built as option 2**: the database stamps `shortfall_applications.gave_to_escheated` as an undo is written (`stamp_shortfall_gave_to_escheated`, every role), reads it again under the credit's lock in `enforce_shortfall_application` (40001 if an escheat committed while the undo waited), and a carry names only such an undo. Option 1 proved costlier than the plan said: a battery transaction can order an undo after an escheat only by the owner choosing times, and every later step on those credits then needs chosen times too. Option 2 is exact in every order and refuses no legitimate flow.

Tests: E15 (the undo and the escheat in one transaction, the two stamps equal: refused), E15a (the stamp, both values), SR10 (the app's carry in a transaction after the escheat's), SR11 (the escheat committing while the undo waits: 40001, the retry with its carry admitted). Mutations M60, M60b, M60c, M60d.
