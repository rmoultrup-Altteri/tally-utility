## For Ryan: the undo a carry names, in one transaction (Fable S1, executed)

r3 requires the undo a carry names to be recorded at or after the escheat. The comparison is `<` on two stamps that are both `now()` inside one transaction. So if an undo (while the credit is active) and the escheat happen in one transaction, a later carry can name that undo for unrelated money (a returned refund's), and the refund's own reissue is then refused. Money is not lost, but the record names the wrong event. Options:

1. `<=`: the undo must be recorded strictly after the escheat, which means a later transaction. Cheap. Refuses one flow: escheating a credit and recording the return, the undo and the carry all in one transaction. Fable judges that flow not legitimate (the escheat is a remittance, the return a bank event). A history load can still choose its times. The battery's E7, E13 and X3 fixtures, which escheat and undo in one transaction, are rebuilt with the owner's chosen times.
2. Record the fact itself: when an undo is inserted, the database stamps whether its credit was then escheated, and a carry may name only such an undo. Exact in every order, one transaction included. Costs a new database-written column on `shortfall_applications`, its guard against the app, and a check that the stamp still matches under the credit's lock.
3. Leave `<` and record the one-transaction case as a residual.

Author's recommendation: option 1.
