## By rule (author)

- **`cash_drawn_at`'s source-shortfall term is observable too (Codex r4 S1; author-run probe 15: 10 at a partial take-back's instant).** r4 said it reads 0 at every take-back's instant. That is false when a source's shortfall application is written in the transaction of a partial take-back under its posting entry: both share `now()`. The comment now says that rows of a take-back's own transaction count at its instant, for both terms. Battery J2 (probe 15) and mutation M64 cover it. The reading was already right; only the comment and the tests change.
- **An undo and its credit's escheat in one statement (Opus r4 S1, executed).** A writable CTE that escheats a credit and undoes its repayment got 40001, "retry", and a retry fails the same way. When the credit's move was made by this transaction (`status_recorded_at = now()`), it is now refused 23514 as the statement's shape: "write the undo in a statement of its own". 40001 stays for an escheat another transaction committed while the undo waited (SR11). Battery E21 (refused) and E21b (separate statements admitted); mutation M66.
- **SR11 checks the SQLSTATE its label names (Codex r4 S2).** The fragment is now `40001: .*was escheated while this undo waited`, read from the verbose error.
- **A sent `gave_to_escheated` is replaced (Fable r4 N1).** E21b sends true on an active credit's undo; E21c reads false.
- **Notes:** the race script's header says its ids collide with -23's battery (Fable r4 N3). The r4 plan's "R1 and R1b stay" was superseded: R0 and R0b test the same dates on an undo recorded alone, and R2 includes "today, admitted" (Opus r4 N1).
