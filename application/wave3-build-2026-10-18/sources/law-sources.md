## Shapes with no source (the invariants sketch them, but no law surveyed requires them)

1. **"Day-26 elderly timing protection" as a disconnect-day bar** (GBM disconnect-eligibility.md:58, :65, rank 7: block when `day_of_month ≥ 26`). **This is a misreading.** §182.002 and §7.45(2)(E)(iv) delay the bill's **payment date to the 25th day after issuance, without penalty**. "26th day" appears only in the applicability test (§182.005(2) / §7.45(2)(E)(i)(I): a utility that "suspends service before the 26th day after the date of the bill"). It counts days after the bill date, not the calendar day of the month. The correct shape is S15, a due-date and late-fee effect, which falls into eligibility only because the bill is not delinquent until day 25. No source supports a calendar day-of-month bar.
2. **Forecast low as the EWE forecast leg** (GBM cold-weather-moratorium.md:27, :47: `forecast_24h_low ≤ 32°F`). The Texas text is "predicted to **remain at or below** that level for the next 24 hours". That is a forecast **maximum** ≤ 32 °F over 24 h, a much rarer condition than a low ≤ 32. A forecast-low input is a **Georgia** shape (515-3-3-.04, strict `<`, 48 h from 8 a.m.), not Texas. Texas needs a forecast max. [u: plain-text reading; no RRC interpretation was found either way.]
3. **"Weekend, holiday, or the day before either"** (disconnect-eligibility.md:57). Texas gas law keys on the **utility's staffed days** (§7.45(4)(F): a day, or the day before a day, when personnel are not available; §104.258(b): weekend unless staffed). A fixed weekend/holiday list matches TX electric §25.29(e), FL and CA. A muni's rule is its own tariff. Shape S10 (a calendar per utility) covers all of these.
4. **Fail-closed when weather data or county is missing** (cold-weather-moratorium.md:49-53, :71). No statute or rule says what to do when data is unavailable. This is a sound design default, not law. Record it as a design rule, not as a law row.
5. **Determination record with expiry, and mandatory re-evaluation at execution** (disconnect-eligibility.md:51, :77). No **Texas** source. The nearest sources are PA §56.94 (door-step abort grounds) and PA §56.91(a) (notice valid 60 days). For a Texas muni, the policy comes only from its tariff.
6. **Reconnecting customers already disconnected when an EWE begins** (cold-weather-moratorium.md:73). No source requires it. §7.460 only prohibits disconnection and defers bills.
7. **Medical-hold IA breach terminating the hold** (texas-collections-overlay.md:48, A12). §7.45(4)(H) requires signing the IA and fixes the hold at 20 days. It says nothing about early termination on breach. §7.45(2)(D)(v) allows disconnect on DPA breach "pursuant to disconnection rules herein", which arguably still respects the 20-day bar. [u] Breach-ends-hold is an inference.
8. **Field protocol: photo, final read, curb shut-off, pay-at-door cancels** (GBM 09:64-71). No Texas source. Only PA §56.94 requires door-step contact and acceptance of tendered payment, and only where the employee is authorized to take payment. Photo documentation: **no source in any jurisdiction surveyed**.
9. **Bypasses for budget billing, payment-posted-not-cleared, and "senior/disabled longer grace"** (GBM 09:51, :58, :60). No surveyed law imposes these. Budget billing appears only as part of the medical duty-to-pay base (PA §56.116). Senior/disabled appears as third-party notice (CA 65+), the PA city-gas winter exception (65+), and the Texas elderly delay (S15). No grace-period extension found.
10. **Third-party notification before disconnect as a Texas requirement** (GBM 01:67-72 "common cadence"). No Texas source. FL (on request), CA (65+/dependent adults), OH (county agency on request) only.
11. **Arrears floor in Texas.** None found (S19 is PA/RI only).
12. **A "pre-disconnect call attempt" step in Texas** (GBM 09:36-37). Not in §7.45. Required in PA (§56.93), NM winter, CA.
