Code § 56.116. Duty of customer to pay bills. 52 Pa. Code § 56.116. Duty of customer to pay bills. § 56.116. Duty of customer to pay bills. Whenever service is restored or termination postponed under the medical emergency procedures, the customer shall retain a duty to make payment on all current undisputed bills or budget billing amount as determined under § 56.12(8) (relating to meter reading; estimated billing; customer readings).
