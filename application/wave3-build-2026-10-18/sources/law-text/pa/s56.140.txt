Code § 56.140. Follow-up response to inquiry. 52 Pa. Code § 56.140. Follow-up response to inquiry. GENERAL PROVISIONS § 56.140. Follow-up response to inquiry. When a customer is waiting for a follow-up response to an initial inquiry under § 56.2 (relating to definitions), termination or threatening termination of service for the subject matter relating to the inquiry in question shall be prohibited until the follow-up response and, when applicable, subsequent dispute resolution is completed by the public utility.
