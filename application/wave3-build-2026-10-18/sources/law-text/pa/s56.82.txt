Code § 56.82. Timing of termination. 52 Pa. Code § 56.82. Timing of termination. § 56.82. Timing of termination. A public utility may terminate service for the reasons in § 56.81 (relating to authorized termination of service) from Monday through Thursday as long as the public utility is able to accept payment to restore service on the day of termination and on the following day and can restore service consistent with § 56.191 (relating to payment and timing).
