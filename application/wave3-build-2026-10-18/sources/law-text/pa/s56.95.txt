Code § 56.95. Deferred termination when no prior contact. 52 Pa. Code § 56.95. Deferred termination when no prior contact. § 56.95. Deferred termination when no prior contact. During the months of December through March, unless personal contact has been made with the customer or responsible adult by personally visiting the customer’s residence, a public utility shall, 48 hours prior to the scheduled date of termination, post a notice of the proposed termination at the service location.
