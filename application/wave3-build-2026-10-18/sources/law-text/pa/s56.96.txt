Code § 56.96. Post-termination notice. 52 Pa. Code § 56.96. Post-termination notice. § 56.96. Post-termination notice. When service is actually terminated, notice that reflects the requirements in § 56.91 (relating to general notice provisions and contents of termination notice) as well as a medical emergency notice in the form which appears in Appendix B (relating to medical emergency notice) shall be conspicuously posted or delivered to a responsible adult person or occupant at the residence of the customer and at the affected premises.
