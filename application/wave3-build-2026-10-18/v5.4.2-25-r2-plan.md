# v5.4.2-25 r2 plan (2026-11-07)

Round 1: Opus and Fable both said not yet; Codex has not reviewed -25 yet. They found the same blocking item. Everything below is settled by a standing rule except item 9, which is Ryan's (asked 2026-11-07).

## Blocking

1. **A record written in the same statement as the resolution is admitted.** (Opus B1, Fable B1.) The "nothing after the resolution" guards (D2e, D4k, D5f) read `disputes.status`, a projection an AFTER trigger writes. So a multi-row insert or writable CTE of a resolution with an escalation, a stage event, a superseding determination or a new amount is accepted, and the reader then reports the later figure for the open period. **Fix (rule: guard the act, not the projection; lesson `projection-checks-lag-in-one-statement`).** Each guard judges openness from `dispute_events`, AFTER ROW: a row whose `record_seq` follows the dispute's resolution or withdrawal is refused. Battery: the multi-row and CTE shapes; a mutation.

## Should fix

2. **Never looser than the law skips duties and stages.** (Opus S1, Fable S2.) The brief says they are compared; the code compares only blocks, subjects, start, end and must-pay. **Fix.** Duties are compared as a superset (as blocks are). A tariff's stages must include every stage the law requires, the regulator's among them. Battery: N1b's fixture is corrected to refuse, with one case per element.
3. **`shelter_ends_on` is not tied to the cited document.** (Fable S1.) A sheltered determination under 16 TAC 7.45 (60 days) with a NULL or 2030 end is accepted. **Fix.** A sheltered determination's end is required, and it is no later than the end the cited document's `shelter_ends` gives for the dispute's bill or received date.
4. **A determination may leave out the utility's own tariff where the law governs.** (Opus S2.) The "cite your tariff" check (D4e) applies only under a delegation, so a determination citing only the law, with its narrower blocks, is accepted though the utility's wider tariff is in force. **Fix (rule: never looser; K-07).** Where a tariff of the utility is in force for the received date, the determination cites it.
5. **No cap on the amount when no lines are named.** (Opus S3.) An amount of 1,000 on a 90 bill is accepted, and the per-line reader shares it out pro rata. **Fix.** The amount is at most the bill's positive charges.
6. **A reading stays `disputed` after its dispute ends.** (Opus S4.) The header says it cannot. **Fix.** The resolution or withdrawal moves the reading out of `disputed` when no other open dispute names it, under the same locks (every naming dispute's, in id order).
7. **Tenancy (R-12).** (Opus S5, Fable S5.) Eleven single-column links between tenant tables: the user stamps, `tariff_id`, the three correction links on events, and the line key. **Fix.** Two-column keys with `tenant_id`; `dispute_tariffs` gains its `(id, tenant_id)` key.
8. **The invariants.** (Fable S3, S4.) -25 adds no guard invariant, and its status move is a SECURITY DEFINER function owned by the superuser.
   - **Invariant.** `assert_dispute_guard_invariants()`, on -24's model: exact triggers, constraints, unique indexes, bodies, attributes, grants and NOT NULL, regenerated from a build.
   - **Status move.** The function is owned by a new NOLOGIN role, `tally_dispute`, with UPDATE on the three status columns only (lesson `seal-pattern-owner-roles`), and is added to the seal invariant.
