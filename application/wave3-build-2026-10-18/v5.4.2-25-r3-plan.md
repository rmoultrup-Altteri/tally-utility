## Blocking

1. **A row given a closing event's id escapes "nothing after the resolution"** (Codex B1, probe 01 executed by the author). `enforce_dispute_nothing_after_close()` excludes `e.id IS DISTINCT FROM NEW.id`, but it runs on amounts and determinations too, whose ids are independent: an amount or determination inserted in the same statement as a withdrawal, carrying the withdrawal's id, is accepted after it. **Fix:** exclude the row itself only on `dispute_events` (the sequence comparison already does); tests for both tables; a mutation.
2. **Two closes of two disputes of one reading can leave the reading disputed** (Codex B2, predicted, probe 02; **executed by Opus S2 and Fable S1** with two sessions). The commit-time release check reads the sibling dispute with no lock, so two overlapping closes each see the other open. Plan item 6 said every naming dispute's lock is taken. **Fix:** the check serialises on the reading (its row lock) before it reads the siblings, so the second close re-reads after the first commits and is refused; both orders of close against the reading's own move; a race (two closes) and a mutation. The lock order in the header says where it sits.

## Should fix

3. **A law that tightens after a tariff is written is recorded as the looser tariff** (Opus S1, executed). D4q requires the tariff wherever one is in force; if a later law row protects more, neither the law alone nor the tariff with the law's blocks is admitted. **Fix (rule: never looser; fail toward protection):** the tariff must be cited only when it compares no looser than the law row cited (`dispute_tariff_compare` empty); otherwise the law is cited. Tests both sides.
4. **Stages are compared by decider only** (Codex S1, probe 03 as predicted; Opus N4). A tariff can stretch the regulator's 15 days to 3,650. **Fix (never looser):** each law stage is matched by a tariff stage of the same decider, in the law's order, whose filing window is no shorter and whose decision window is no longer (`none` on either side compared in the protective direction; a pair not comparable is refused, as elsewhere). Tests per element; N9b's mutation extended.
5. **The record sequence's grants are not pinned** (Fable S2, executed with a stray grant). The "nothing after" rule orders by `dispute_record_seq`; with UPDATE on it granted to the application, every invariant passes and an amount is admitted after a resolution. **Fix:** the guard invariant pins the sequence's ACL and owner; a test and a mutation.
6. **D4f's fixture is gone when it runs** (Codex S2). Keep a real foreign tariff; test the not-visible case and the owner-visible tenant, state, service and system mismatches separately; mutate each comparison.
