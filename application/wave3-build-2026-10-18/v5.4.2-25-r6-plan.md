## Must fix

1. **The law's final flag reaches the combined stages only when the stages section has a finding** (Codex B1, Fable N6; confirmed on the real path by probe 00). Take a policy with 7.45's two stages, then its own governing-body appeal: by L2d its regulator is `final: false`. Against any later law whose stages it already meets, there is no stages finding. So the combined record keeps the regulator not final, even though the law makes the regulator's decision final. A finding elsewhere (an added block) does not help. Only a tightened regulator deadline does, because that sets off the merge. **Fix:** the stages merge runs whenever the law has stages and the tariff's stages are not identical to the merged result. More simply, it always runs where the law governs. A tariff no looser in its stages then comes back with the law's final flag at each of the law's places and nothing else changed. **Tests:** D9a's fixture under a law with the same stage terms, and under a law that only adds a block: the regulator is final in both. Mutation: the merge only on a finding.
2. **"Undisputed charges" is ranked above every withholding cap** (Codex B2, Opus S1; confirmed by probes 00 and 11). "Pay the undisputed charges" means the customer withholds exactly the disputed amount, so it is a withholding cap of 1. Against a cap above 1 (the Government Code 2251 shape, 1.1, the X8 fixture), it asks for more. Today a policy of undisputed charges is admitted under a 1.1 law, while a cap of 1, which asks the same amount, is refused. In the other direction, a 1.1 policy against an undisputed-charges law is called looser, so a more generous policy is refused at filing and loses to the law in a combined record. **Fix (by rule):** against `withhold_cap`, `undisputed_charges` compares as a fraction of 1, in both directions: equal at 1, looser below the law's fraction, more protective above it. The design's ordering paragraph changes with it. Against the other strategies the ranking stands (undisputed charges asks no more than an average cap or the full bill). **Tests:** filing, both directions, at fractions 0.9, 1 and 1.1 (N7d's twin with undisputed charges), and a later-law combination in both directions. Mutations on each leg.
