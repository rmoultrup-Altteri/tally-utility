## As built (r2, 2026-11-27)

Every item above is in the patch; its header lists them ("r2 (round 1's findings)") and the residuals R1, R6, R7 carry the notes. Choices made in the build, by rule:

- **Item 2's lock.** Rather than take several subject locks in an order, the subject's advisory lock no longer includes the meter: every subject at one location (the location-wide one and each meter's) shares one lock, so an act reads all their live decisions with none in flight, and no second lock order is needed. Contention is per customer, act, bill and location, which is negligible.
- **Item 2's reach.** The check binds only the act itself (`p_live`), not a log line: a disconnection already carried out is still logged.
- **Item 4.** `dunning_events.record_seq` (the shared record sequence) orders a bill's steps; the stage a step moves from is the latest stage-moving step's, read under the bill's row lock. A BEFORE trigger sees the rows an earlier part of its own statement inserted, which is what makes a multi-row insert correct.
- **Item 5.** The approver must be an active `tenant_admin` of the order's own utility. `session_is_supervisor()` (-13) is unchanged, since other gates use it; -26 no longer calls it. A T1 supervisor writing an approval for T2 is now refused by this check (42501, `[F3a]`) before row security, reading nothing of T2's (battery H3 updated).
- **Item 6.** From a stage off the ladder (payment plan, resolved, written off) an escalation step moves the bill to its own stage: collections resumed. Battery A8c and A8h now expect the bill to stay where it was.
- **Item 7.** A late fee is gated under `late_fee` and `penalty`; a `late_fee` or `penalty` bill line must name a gated charge of the bill's customer; a charge cannot become or stop being a late fee. The precondition also stops on such charges and lines. `battery-21`'s late-fee line (A5) now bills a gated charge, so `battery-21` needs -26 (as `ci.sh` runs it).
- **Item 9.** An execution is not dated before its order or its decision was recorded, nor in the future. A battery transaction stamps everything at one instant, so the battery's executions now arrive at `now()` under decisions for today; the races likewise.
- **Item 11.** `assert_collections_guard_invariants()` pins -26's triggers (ENABLE ALWAYS), constraints, unique indexes, table grants and row security, and its functions' bodies, settings and grants; `tests/v5.4.2-26/fill-manifest.py` writes the lists. The patch's last block calls it and every earlier patch's.
- **Item 15 (Codex S3).** The mutation runner no longer counts a race leg that failed for want of a lock wait, or because session A failed, as a catch.

**Not done, and why:** the "decision recorded before the act" bound (Opus S3) is applied to executions only. For a charge's or a step's date it would refuse every history load and every battery fixture dated before the transaction, and C3h's reason (no relying on an old evaluation at execution) is about executions.
