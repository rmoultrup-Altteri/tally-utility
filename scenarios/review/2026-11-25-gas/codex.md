## Findings

1. **Blocking — SCN-0137, lines 172–185: all three March totals omit the annual pipeline safety fee.** BB-08 is a commercial customer, not an exempt state agency. `scenarios/cast.md:75` requires $1.00 per service line on the March bill. `scenarios/tax.md:352,354,366` explicitly excludes this fee from the sales-tax base. The gas components and taxes shown are correct, but the complete totals must be **$454.91, $458.38 and $451.42**, assuming one service line. **Fix:** state one service line, add the $1.00 non-taxable fee in each column and increase each total by $1.00. Alternatively explicitly make this a gas-components subtotal, not the bill total.

2. **Blocking — SCN-0140, line 245: the October re-read precedes the original read and bill.** An Oct 12 request cannot discover that the Oct 15 reading on an already-issued bill was keyed wrong. Pecan Flat normally issues on the 20th (`scenarios/cast.md:122`). **Fix:** move the request/re-read after issuance, for example Oct 22, 2027, and state that the original bill issued Oct 20. The March and August requests need no change.

3. **Blocking — SCN-0142, lines 276,286–287, and cast line 136: the built cap blocks at the third estimate, not first at the fourth.** `sql/tu.sql:17363` tests `v_count + 1 >= v_cap`; `sql/tu.sql:16865` supplies the default 3. The latest parity description, `application/invariant-parity-measurement-2026-10-13.md:422`, also says approval is refused **at** the cap. Therefore the April estimate raises the exception unless resolved; it cannot simply become the third billed estimate as written. The April bill used by part (e) is missing that prerequisite. **Fix:** make (c) raise and resolve the exception explicitly before issuing the April bill, and show the renewed exception in (d). If the intended policy really is three freely permitted estimates, label it a proposed change to the existing guard and obtain the ruling instead of calling that behavior built. Also distinguish the existing resolution paths: `sql/tu.sql:17112` allows a recorded override or dispatched field order; it does not require a completed actual reading in every case.

4. **Blocking — SCN-0142, lines 279–285: the prior-year month is inconsistent with Gulf Plains' billing calendar.** `scenarios/cast.md:82` says the Feb 1 read and Feb 5 bill cover January. Estimating the same month last year therefore needs **January 2026** usage, whereas the Given supplies February 2026's 9.0 Mcf. The $82.50 arithmetic is correct for 9.0 Mcf, but that quantity is not derived from the stated method and period. **Fix:** supply January 2026 usage of 9.0 Mcf (including migrated history if needed), or explicitly invent a different method indexed by read month. Supply the March/April estimate inputs too if those bills are to be executable fixtures. Split the returned-card and no-card branches: the unconditional streak of 2 at line 285 cannot be asserted for both while line 289 leaves the returned-card reset rule open.

5. **Blocking — SCN-0142, line 277: proposed cause classifications are presented as Kyle's ruling.** The cited R-30 is in `GBM/application/kyle-decisions-2026-09-02-a2-backbilling-caps.md:287–321`, but its own issue date is **Sep 14**, and it expressly says at line 312 that the bucket assignments are an engineering proposal, not Kyle's ruling. Kyle settled derivation from contemporaneous evidence and the protective default. The scenario instead attributes the particular dog/gate/offline classifications to Kyle on Sep 2. **Fix:** cite the dated R-30 subsection accurately; distinguish the ruled mechanism from proposed cause mapping. Mark the specific mapping provisional or cite a subsequent decision that actually adopts it. Retain the more-protective outcome when evidence conflicts or is absent.

6. **Blocking — SCN-0142, lines 277–288: the stated legal disconnection rule omits an express exception.** Section 7.45(4)(E)(vii) also excepts bills rendered under an approved meter-reading plan. Saying an estimated bill cannot support disconnection “unless” access was beyond the utility's control is incomplete. The cited R-30 itself discusses this separate, still-unmodelled route (`GBM/application/kyle-decisions-2026-09-02-a2-backbilling-caps.md:319`). **Fix:** add “Gulf Plains has no approved meter-reading plan” to the Given and describe the rule with both exceptions. The postcard statement should also retain the condition that the meter can be read by the customer without significant inconvenience or special equipment; give GP-08 that fact. State the unincorporated-area scope in the common legal summary at line 10. Evidence: [16 TAC §7.45, opening text and (4)(E)(vii), (6)(C)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/texas/16-Tex-Admin-Code-SS-7-45).

7. **Blocking — common rule line 7 and SCN-0130 line 37: “every other rate” at period end contradicts the existing split-rate rule.** The snapshot's period-end coordinate is not a rule to discard intra-period rate changes. `scenarios/billing-pipeline-and-charge-composition.md:8–38` (SCN-0001) applies separate September and October customer/distribution rates; `scenarios/cancel-rebill.md:233` says a correction repeats that original split. Kyle's Nov 3 answer 1 permits split, read-date or cycle methods, chosen when the change is entered. `application/APPLICATION-CONTRACTS.md:216` binds the snapshot coordinate, not a replacement for that billing policy. **Fix:** say the other charges follow the original period's applicable rate-change method, while the gas cost uses its billing-month key. In SCN-0130, state simply that no other rate changes within either period.

8. **Should-fix — SCN-0137, lines 170–190: the cycle result lacks the policy that selects the old factor.** The split and read-date columns follow from the dates. “By cycle” alone does not establish why a March 16 bill uses 1.0400 rather than 1.0600. In SCN-0001, a cycle spanning a change uses the new rate (`scenarios/billing-pipeline-and-charge-composition.md:38`). This is not necessarily the wrong factor, but the fixture leaves the decisive fact unstated. **Fix:** explicitly say which cycle first receives the new factor, or define cycle selection by the period's opening date as an invented provisional choice. Retain the Open: the cited `GBM/application/configurable-rules/decision-tables/cross-month-factor-application.md:14,35–43` is design material advocating time weighting, not a Kyle ruling authorizing these three options.

9. **Should-fix — SCN-0133 line 88; SCN-0136 line 153; SCN-0144 line 310: separate existing records from missing enforcement.** The rate-basis election is built, but refusal of current rates for gas is core behavior (`scenarios/cancel-rebill.md:147,159`; `sql/tu.sql:21136–21139` still describes current/custom elections). Zone and pressure confirmation stamps already exist (`sql/tu.sql:12099–12121`), as does the swap mismatch confirmation/reason record (`sql/tu.sql:12124–12138`). The missing zone model and pressure comparison are real gaps; describing the whole pressure check as “not built” obscures that substrate. **Fix:** explicitly label the gas-only refusal as the core's, and say confirmation records exist while zone resolution and the post-test comparison/activation gate remain unbuilt core work. SCN-0136, SCN-0137 and SCN-0135 should similarly identify their proposed refusal's owner rather than leaving a future test to infer whether it exercises setup, billing or a database guard.

10. **Should-fix — SCN-0136, lines 154–162: activation without confirmation conflicts with the cited activation gate, and the billed volume lacks an opening read.** D3B-2 requires measurement-critical facts affirmed at activation (`GBM/application/wu5-wu6-kyle-decisions-2026-07-10.md:125–126`). The Given nevertheless activates BB-18 unconfirmed, then waits until billing to detect it. Also “the Dec 15 read is 30.0 Mcf” is an index value unless its initial reading is supplied. **Fix:** make activation fail until confirmation, or clearly identify this as a legacy/bypassed-gate case. Give an installation reading of 0.0, or say 30.0 is the raw consumption difference. State the initial billing period if the scenario is meant to assert an issued bill rather than just converted volume.

11. **Should-fix — SCN-0132, lines 81–82: the refund citation and undercharge Open point to the wrong scope.** SCN-0015 is Gulf Plains' fast-meter refund within a bounded meter-test window (`scenarios/backbilling.md:69–81`); it does not establish unlimited city refunds. Bluebonnet's applicable rule is SCN-0019 and Kyle Nov 3 answer 8 / Nov 5 answers 10–12 (`scenarios/backbilling.md:152–164`). SCN-0027(a)'s regulated-utility rate-error path does not itself displace the silent city's six-month default; SCN-0028 applies that default to a Bluebonnet rate correction (`scenarios/cancel-rebill.md:214–226`). **Fix:** cite SCN-0019 for refund reach, apply the city default to a mistaken-factor undercharge, and keep only the genuinely unsettled locked-factor correction mechanism Open. Also qualify the common “rebill uses the figure the original bill used” at line 6 with the SCN-0132 exception for correcting that very figure.

12. **Should-fix — SCN-0145, lines 329–336: the later test's scope does not establish that PF-07 is unaffected.** A test performed after reuse could implicate both deployments; meter ownership alone does not limit the correction to PF-06. SCN-0037 explicitly evaluates every applicable period for the customer and premise that received its bill (`scenarios/backbilling.md:254–264`). “Was slow before its removal” establishes earlier error but does not establish that refurbishment fixed it. The Given also never installs the replacement meter whose readings line 335 expects. **Fix:** add a documented successful post-refurbishment test, its date and the evidence bounding the earlier defect, and install/name PF-06's replacement meter on Mar 15. Then “never to PF-07” and the address-history assertion have reproducible premises.

13. **Should-fix — coverage gaps across SCN-0130–0145, especially lines 263–289 and 298–304.** The family has a cap sequence but no failed-validation actual that must *not* reset it, no backdated actual preserving later estimates, and no below-cap overdue six-month actual-read check. These are distinct from counting estimates: Kyle D14-1 (`GBM/application/wu5-wu6-kyle-decisions-2026-07-10.md:106`) and `sql/tu.sql:16890–16916` make validation and reading dates decisive; parity CI-090 says the six-month check is the core's (`application/invariant-parity-measurement-2026-10-13.md:399`). There is also no base-pressure meter wrongly given an elevated factor (CI-029, parity line 338), or missing/overlapping dated factor for an otherwise confirmed zone (CI-026). **Fix:** add concise variants using GP-08 and an existing Bluebonnet residential customer. For gas cost, add a month-boundary delayed issue/final bill and a legitimate zero figure, distinguishing zero from absent. These test the new selection/refusal rules without requiring new utilities, energy-unit tariffs or weather normalization.

14. **Nit — cast line 138 versus SCN-0139 line 227: make the residential meter exception visible in the shared cast.** The cast states Pecan Flat's residential meters have four-dial Ccf indexes; SCN-0139 explicitly invents a Mcf meter for PF-01 and describes Ccf as merely the majority. This is clearly a deliberate local override, so it does not invalidate the calculation, but a shared cast fixture would choose the wrong unit. **Fix:** write “normally” in the cast and name PF-01's scenario exception. Likewise, distinguish the raw consumption difference from the cumulative “raw reading” where factors multiply a volume.
