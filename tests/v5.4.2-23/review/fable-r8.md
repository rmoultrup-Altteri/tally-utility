## Should fix

### S1. agree() states "no more than" where the exact per-line cap is provable; with the own cap untested, no mutation now anchors at agree() (executed, P-A)

What each line can pay is exactly **least(its own cap, the pool)**: for a credit, `customer_credit_own_remaining_as_of`; for the payment, `received − applied − refunded − (K.amount − the shares as recorded)`; the pool, the sum of the positive positions (equivalently the payment less every draw and take-back). I executed, as tally_app, for every active line of all 26 agree() pairs and the P-G family: a refund of least(own, pool) admitted and a cent more refused (or a cent refused when the least is ≤ 0), and the position never above greatest(that, 0). 27 of 27 PASS; sample readings: Q1a "credits 3322 20 of own 20, 3323 20 of own 30, pool 40"; Q2a "payment 40 of own 40, 3361 0 of own −10, 3362 10 of own 20, pool 50"; Z1a "3221 −30 of own −50, 3222 0 of own 20, pool 0" (the covering credit pays nothing: the pool binds). The refund is dated after every fixture row, so the cap's binding date is the last one; agree() already relies on that.

agree_side today tests only pool + 0.01 and the position. Two gaps follow. (a) A line reading 0 beside a positive pool (K in Q2a, Q3b; the escheated K in Q4c) has its own cap untested: a mutation admitting K to refund 10 while reading 0 would pass, as N207 did. (b) The runner's anchor list has no mutation at any agree() label (checked: the anchors are reads checks, refuses_at checks and APPLY); agree() catches nothing in the suite now, and Y7j2 covers one shape of one cap. **Fix:** give agree_side the exact cap (least(own, pool)) as the admitted figure and the cap plus a cent as the refused one, keeping the three other legs; the header's statement can then say the exact thing ("a line pays exactly the lesser of its own cap and the pool; its position is an attribution of at most that"). My `agree_exact` in `probe-r8.sql` is that function. One round's test change, no patch change.
