## Notes

- **N1 (executed PA1–PA3c; predicted).** *The overpayment-credit half of the skip is harmless.* At an overpayment credit's insert, the payment's own cap counts: the payment's other draws + the credit's amount + the rows under the posting entry − the family's shares as recorded. The together-cap counts: the payment's other draws + the family credits' draws (none: the credit is new and alone) + the rows under the entry. The difference is the credit's amount less its share, which is never negative (`overpayment_shares` caps the short-side share at the credit's amount), so the payment's own cap is the tighter of the two at that instant. PA1 (refund 100, take-back 30, then a credit of 1: refused "R-06 cap"), PA2 (refund 50, take-back 60, credit of 1: refused) executed. PA3a is the pre-existing r5 shape, not r9's: with the family holding 20 (refund 50, take-back 30), a credit of 21 is admitted and reads 20 (the take-back's excess is placed on it: "it was sized after the row"), a credit of 51 refused; agree() passes and nothing beyond 20 is payable. The record shows a credit of 21 for a hold of 20; the positions are right. Unchanged since r5; noted, not a finding.
- **N2 (predicted).** *Every input of `overpayment_shares` that a later writer could move is now ordered.* The function reads, per take-back: each credit's `created_at` (fixed from insert), its void stamp (`status_recorded_at`, now the leaving check; it changes only with the status, r6), its draws at the take-back's instant (`cash_drawn_at`: applications and their undos, reissues, donations and their undos, remittances — each a draw or undo through `check_cash_source`, whose `check_cash_order` call at 3355 runs before the undo branch and carries the arrival entry, so none is stamped before a row under the entry), and its refunds that have since left (`status_recorded_at`, the leaving check). The payment side reads the same kinds. An escheat move enters no share (only `voided` ends a hold); its remittance is a draw. A give-back is itself a row under the entry, ordered against the tree by the tree lock. Nothing else writes those columns. The check is under the payment's lock in both callers (2496/2498 before 2504; 2602/2609 before 2632), the lock every row under the entry takes at 899 before its own `check_cash_order`, so a take-back is either committed and seen or waits.

- **N4 (predicted; the battery's 35 agree() sites executed).** *agree()'s payment cap is the cap's own.* `check_cash_source`'s payment-side cap (3382–3407, `NOT v_family`) is: applications + refunds pending or posted + the live overpayment credit's amount + the rows under the posting entry net of the family's shares, against the amount, from the posting entry's date. With K voided the cap has no `v_split` and `cash_source_draws` leaves the voided credit out; the battery subtracts 0.
